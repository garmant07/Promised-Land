Bible Quiz Questions – Old Testament – Volume 5: Wisdom and Love Poetry
Proverbs–Song of Solomon · 51 chapters · 408 questions
Collection index and Volume 1
Each chapter has eight questions: three Easy, three Medium and two Hard. The question order and multiple-choice answer positions are mixed. Difficulty is editorial: Hard questions require closer recall, comparison of passages or careful interpretation.
Quotation questions marked WEB use the World English Bible. Other questions preserve the study material’s wording or paraphrase a passage. Accept a clearly equivalent answer when exact wording is not being tested.
Read the passage in context before applying it today or connecting it to Christ. A character’s statement is not automatically the narrator’s conclusion. Theological questions use the course summaries listed below alongside Scripture; disputed readings are identified in the explanations.
