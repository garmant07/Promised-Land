Bible Quiz Questions – New Testament
Collection index and Volume 1: Gospels
27 books · 260 chapters · 2,080 questions across three linked volumes


Each chapter has eight questions: three Easy, three Medium and two Hard. Question order and multiple-choice answer positions are mixed. Hard questions use closer recall, comparison of passages or careful interpretation.
Easy = green · Medium = yellow · Hard = red


Quotation questions marked WEB follow the World English Bible. Interpretation questions paraphrase the passage; accept a clearly equivalent answer when exact wording is not being tested.
Read each passage in its literary and historical setting. Theological explanations draw on Scripture and the Gospel Worldview course summary. Course-specific views and disputed readings are identified rather than presented as universal agreement. A character’s words are not automatically the narrator’s teaching.
